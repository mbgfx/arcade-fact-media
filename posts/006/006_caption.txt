Pac-Man's ghosts don't all chase you the same way. 🕹️

Blinky chases you directly. Pinky and Inky try to get in front of you to corner you. Clyde switches between chasing and running away, depending on how close he is.

🔎 Bonus: Their names are Blinky, Pinky, Inky and Clyde, and learning how each one moves is the secret to surviving later levels.
