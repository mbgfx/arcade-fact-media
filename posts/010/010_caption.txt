The clouds and bushes in Super Mario Bros. are the same thing. 🕹️

To save space on a tiny cartridge, the bushes in the background use the same sprite as the clouds, just recoloured green.

🔎 Bonus: The whole game had to fit in about 40 KB, so reusing graphics was essential.
