Space Invaders' difficulty curve was a happy accident. 🕹️

The aliens speed up as you shoot them because the hardware could draw fewer aliens faster. Designer Tomohiro Nishikado noticed and decided to keep it as a gameplay mechanic.

🔎 Bonus: The background beat speeds up with the invaders too. Critics have compared it to a racing heartbeat.
