Nintendo has been around since 1889. 🕹️

Nintendo was founded on 23 September 1889 by craftsman Fusajiro Yamauchi to make handmade hanafuda playing cards. Video games came almost a century later.

🔎 Bonus: By the 1960s Nintendo was also making toys, including the Ultra Hand, a grabbing arm toy.
