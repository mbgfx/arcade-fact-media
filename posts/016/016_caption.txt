Mario's moustache and cap were a tech workaround. 🕹️

With so few pixels, a mouth was hard to show, so the moustache implies one. The cap means his hair never had to be animated. Graphical limits created one of gaming's most famous looks.

🔎 Bonus: Donkey Kong was Mario's very first appearance, back when he was still called Jumpman.
