Sega's name is short for something. 🕹️

Sega stands for SErvice GAmes. Service Games of Japan supplied coin-operated slot machines to US military bases, and 'Sega' first appeared on a slot machine called Diamond Star in 1954.

🔎 Bonus: The company we know as Sega was founded as Nihon Goraku Bussan on 3 June 1960.
