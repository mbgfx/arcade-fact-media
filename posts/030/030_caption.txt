'Warrior needs food, badly.' 🕹️

Gauntlet's narrator constantly warned players as their health drained away. The cabinet was four-player only at launch, and a coin could revive a fallen hero with full health.

🔎 Bonus: To stop players simply buying their way to the top, each final score was divided by the number of credits used.
