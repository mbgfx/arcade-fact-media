Q*bert's swearing is total gibberish. 🕹️

Instead of real words, the speech chip strings together random sounds, shown as '@!#?@!'. That symbol string was even used as the game's name on some early test machines.

🔎 Bonus: During development the game was called Cubes, and one rejected title was 'Snots And Boogers'.
