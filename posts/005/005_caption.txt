Mario's first name wasn't Mario. 🕹️

In Donkey Kong the hero was called Jumpman. He was later named Mario after the landlord of Nintendo of America's original office space.

🔎 Bonus: In the original Japanese arcade release he had no name at all. Pauline, the woman he rescues, was named after the wife of Nintendo of America's warehouse manager.
