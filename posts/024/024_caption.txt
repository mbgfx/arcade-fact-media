Berzerk's unkillable villain was named after an office manager. 🕹️

Evil Otto, the bouncing smiley face that hunts you through walls, is named after Dave Otto, office manager at Dave Nutting Associates, who brought in rules the engineers disliked.

🔎 Bonus: Berzerk was also one of the earliest games to use speech synthesis, with robots taunting the player.
