Mortal Kombat helped create game age ratings. 🕹️

Concerns over Mortal Kombat and Night Trap led to US congressional hearings in 1992 and 1993. The industry was given one year to form a rating system, which led to the ESRB.

🔎 Bonus: On the SNES, Mortal Kombat's blood was swapped for sweat.
