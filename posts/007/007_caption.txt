Atari once buried hundreds of thousands of games in the desert. 🕹️

In 1983 Atari dumped cartridges, including E.T., in a landfill in Alamogordo, New Mexico. A former manager put it at about 728,000. When it was dug up in 2014, only around 1,300 were removed.

🔎 Bonus: For years the dump was treated as an urban legend, with claims of millions of cartridges, until the 2014 dig confirmed it.
