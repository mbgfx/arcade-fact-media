This Game Boy survived a bombing, and still worked. 🕹️

Medic Stephan Scoggins' Game Boy was badly burnt when his barracks were bombed in the Gulf War. It still switched on. Nintendo displayed it at its New York store, playing Tetris, until it was retired in 2023.

🔎 Bonus: Nintendo gave its owner, Stephan Scoggins, a replacement Game Boy.
