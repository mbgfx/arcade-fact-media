Metroid's hero hid a twist until the very end. 🕹️

Metroid shows one of five endings depending on how fast you finish. In the three best ones, Samus Aran removes her suit and is revealed to be a woman, a big surprise for players in 1986.

🔎 Bonus: The idea came partway through development, when one developer asked if it would be cool if the person inside the suit turned out to be a woman.
