Pac-Man almost had a very different name. 🕹️

In Japan the game launched as Puck Man. Before the US release, Namco worried vandals would scratch the P on the cabinets into an F, so the name was changed to Pac-Man.

🔎 Bonus: Pac-Man was location-tested in Tokyo in May 1980 and reached North America that October through Midway.
