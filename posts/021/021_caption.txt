Donkey Kong was made to rescue a flop. 🕹️

Nintendo's Radar Scope failed in the US and left a pile of unsold cabinets. Donkey Kong was created so those machines could be converted into a new game, and it became a massive hit.

🔎 Bonus: Radar Scope came out in 1980. Donkey Kong arrived in 1981 and introduced the world to Mario.
