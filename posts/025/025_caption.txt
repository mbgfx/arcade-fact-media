On the SNES, Mortal Kombat's blood became sweat. 🕹️

Nintendo's version swapped the blood for sweat. The Sega Genesis version hid the blood behind a code that spelled ABACABB, and it became the best-selling console game of 1993.

🔎 Bonus: Concerns over violent games like Mortal Kombat led to US congressional hearings in 1992 and 1993.
