One of gaming's first famous Easter eggs hid in Adventure. 🕹️

Atari didn't credit its programmers, so Warren Robinett hid a secret room in Adventure showing his name. Players found it only after release, by carrying a single-pixel Gray Dot to the right spot.

🔎 Bonus: Fixing the ROM would have cost around $10,000, so Atari left it in and dubbed such hidden features 'Easter eggs'. It wasn't the very first, but it popularised the idea.
