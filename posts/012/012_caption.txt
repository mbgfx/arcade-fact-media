Atari is named after a move in a board game. 🕹️

'Atari' is a term from the game Go, for a stone or group about to be captured. It's like saying 'check' in chess. Founders Nolan Bushnell and Ted Dabney picked it after their first choice, Syzygy, was taken.

🔎 Bonus: Atari was incorporated in California on 27 June 1972.
