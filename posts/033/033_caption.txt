Pac-Man holds a world record for cabinets. 🕹️

Guinness World Records recognises Pac-Man for the most coin-operated arcade machines installed worldwide: 293,822.

🔎 Bonus: The Pac-Man series holds eight Guinness World Records, including Most Successful Coin-Operated Game.
