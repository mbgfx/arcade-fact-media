The first home console began life as a 'Brown Box'. 🕹️

Ralph Baer's prototype console was nicknamed the Brown Box because it was wrapped in brown tape to look like wood veneer. Magnavox licensed it and released it in 1972 as the Odyssey, the first home video game console.

🔎 Bonus: Baer is often called the father of video games. In 2006 he donated his hardware prototypes and documents to the Smithsonian Institution.
