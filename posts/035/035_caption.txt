There's a perfect score in Pac-Man. 🕹️

The highest possible Pac-Man score is 3,333,360 points. Reaching it means playing through every level without missing a single dot, energiser, ghost or bonus fruit.

🔎 Bonus: The first publicly verified perfect game was played by Billy Mitchell on 3 July 1999.
