The first arcade video game came out in 1971. 🕹️

Computer Space, made by Nolan Bushnell and Ted Dabney and released by Nutting Associates, was the first arcade video game. It came in a futuristic fibreglass cabinet and sold about 1,300 to 1,500 units.

🔎 Bonus: A white Computer Space cabinet appeared in the 1973 film Soylent Green, the first appearance of a video game in a movie.
