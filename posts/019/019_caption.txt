Tetris is named after tennis. Sort of. 🕹️

Alexey Pajitnov combined 'tetra' (four, for the four-block pieces) with 'tennis', his favourite sport. The first version ran on a Soviet Elektronika 60, which had no graphics, so the blocks were drawn with brackets.

🔎 Bonus: According to The Tetris Company, Tetris has sold more than 520 million copies across all versions.
