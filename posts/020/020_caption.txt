Missile Command gave its creator nightmares. 🕹️

As the deadline approached, designer Dave Theurer had nightmares about nuclear blasts. You can't win the game. When you lose, it doesn't say Game Over. It says THE END.

🔎 Bonus: It was released in June 1980, at the height of the Cold War.
