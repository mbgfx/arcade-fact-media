Q*bert cabinets had a pinball part inside. 🕹️

When Q*bert fell off the pyramid, a pinball 'knocker' inside the cabinet made a loud thump. The designers had wanted foam padding too, but it was too expensive.

🔎 Bonus: Q*bert reached North American arcades in October 1982.
