Was Pac-Man really inspired by pizza? 🕹️

Toru Iwatani often told the pizza-with-a-missing-slice story, but later said it was only partly true. Pac-Man's shape also came from simplifying 'kuchi', the Japanese character for mouth.

🔎 Bonus: Pac-Man reached North American arcades in October 1980 through Midway.
