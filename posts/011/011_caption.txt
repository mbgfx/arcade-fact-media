Did Space Invaders really cause a coin shortage in Japan? 🕹️

Probably not. The famous story says the game caused a 100-yen coin shortage, but researchers found Japan Mint output didn't change, and the Bank of Japan had no evidence for it. It's an arcade myth.

🔎 Bonus: Taito never denied the coin shortage story, which helped the myth spread.
