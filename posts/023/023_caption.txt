Asteroids players found a way to score forever. 🕹️

'Lurking' players left just one or two rocks in play and hid at the screen edge, picking off flying saucers for points with little risk. Atari issued a patched chip to stop it.

🔎 Bonus: Asteroids drew its graphics as lines on a vector monitor and became Atari's best-selling arcade game, with over 70,000 units sold.
