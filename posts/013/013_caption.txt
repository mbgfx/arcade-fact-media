Pac-Man has a final level you can't finish. 🕹️

Level 256 is a 'kill screen'. An integer overflow makes the level load improperly, scrambling the maze with garbled graphics so it can't be cleared.

🔎 Bonus: The highest possible score, reached by playing every level perfectly, is 3,333,360 points.
