Pong was only meant to be a practice project. 🕹️

Nolan Bushnell gave engineer Allan Alcorn the job as a warm-up training exercise, reportedly telling him it was for a General Electric contract. It became Atari's first hit.

🔎 Bonus: The prototype went into a bar called Andy Capp's Tavern in August 1972, and broke from too many quarters.
