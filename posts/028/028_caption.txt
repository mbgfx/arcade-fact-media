Super Mario Bros. is smaller than a phone photo. 🕹️

The whole game fits in about 40 kilobytes: 32 KB of code and data plus 8 KB of graphics. A single photo on your phone is usually a hundred times bigger.

🔎 Bonus: Super Mario Bros. has sold more than 58 million copies including re-releases.
