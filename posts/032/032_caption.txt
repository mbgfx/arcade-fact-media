Space Invaders turned home consoles into must-haves. 🕹️

The Atari 2600 version was the first official licence of an arcade game for a console. It quadrupled the system's sales, becoming the first 'killer app'.

🔎 Bonus: Designer Tomohiro Nishikado wasn't credited at first, because staying anonymous was part of his contract.
