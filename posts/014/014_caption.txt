Ms. Pac-Man started life as a hack. 🕹️

Programmers at General Computer Corporation built an enhancement kit for Pac-Man called Crazy Otto, without Namco's consent. Midway bought the rights, and it was reworked into Ms. Pac-Man.

🔎 Bonus: Ms. Pac-Man hit arcades in February 1982 and became one of the most successful American arcade games ever made.
