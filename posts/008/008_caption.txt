Combos were never meant to be in Street Fighter II. 🕹️

During a bug check on the car bonus stage, the team noticed some moves flowed straight into others. They decided to leave it in as a hidden feature, and combos went on to define fighting games.

🔎 Bonus: Producer Noritaka Funamizu later said of it: "A bug? Maybe." Capcom sold more than 60,000 Street Fighter II arcade machines.
