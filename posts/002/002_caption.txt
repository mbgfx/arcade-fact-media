The first Pong machine broke because it was too popular. 🕹️

Atari's Pong prototype went into Andy Capp's Tavern in August 1972. Soon it stopped working. The fault? The coin mechanism was overflowing with quarters.

🔎 Bonus: Pong was built by engineer Allan Alcorn, and it became Atari's first big hit.
